# The Price of History — brand guide

Instagram channel: stories of money and history, told in torn paper.

## Name and mark
- **Name:** The Price of History (suggested handle: @thepriceofhistory; check it's free on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube)
- **Mark:** a dark torn-paper **price tag** (pointed left end, punched hole) with the name in cream capitals. Used on every end card.
- **Font:** Fraunces (free, Google Fonts). ExtraBold 800 for titles and the wordmark, letter-spacing about 4 px; Italic 400 for small lines.

## Colours
| Use | Hex |
|---|---|
| Ink / tag | `#231a16` |
| Cream paper | `#efe6cf` |
| Gold | `#e8b33a` / `#c98b1f` |
| Deep teal | `#2f4f4a` |
| Brick red (one accent) | `#a8382b` |

## Logo prompt (for ChatGPT, Midjourney, Firefly or Ideogram)

### Main emblem (no text: add the name yourself)
```
A logo emblem in a handmade torn-paper collage style. An antique price tag cut from dark brown-black paper (#231a16), with a pointed left end and a round punched hole, tied with a short piece of rough twine. The tag hangs from an old gold coin cut from metallic gold paper (#e8b33a), with a simple engraved laurel ring around the coin's edge. Every piece has a ragged torn edge with a thin white fibrous rim where the paper tore, visible paper grain and a gentle crumple, and casts a soft short drop shadow on the layer below, like real paper pieces laid on a desk. Behind them, a torn circle of deep teal paper (#2f4f4a) on a warm cream paper background (#efe6cf). One small brick-red accent (#a8382b): the twine. Flat, warm, vintage storybook look. Centered, symmetrical, generous empty space, clean silhouette that reads clearly at small sizes. No text, no letters, no numbers.
```
Settings: square 1:1, 1024×1024 or larger (Midjourney: `--ar 1:1 --style raw`).

### Profile-picture version
Add to the end of the prompt: `the emblem fills the whole frame, the teal circle touching the edges`. Instagram crops profile pictures to a circle and shows them at about 110 px.

### Alternative idea
```
Same torn-paper collage style and colours: an antique hourglass cut from layered paper, its sand made of tiny gold paper coins falling from the top bulb to the bottom, a small dark price tag tied to the hourglass's frame with red twine. Cream background, no text.
```

### Things to avoid (negative prompt)
```
text, letters, watermark, signature, photorealistic metal, 3D render, glossy plastic, neon, digital gradients, busy background, faces, real brand logos
```

## Wordmark (add in Canva or Photoshop)
- "THE PRICE OF HISTORY" in **Fraunces ExtraBold**, cream `#efe6cf` on the dark tag, letter-spacing +4.
- For a stacked version: "THE PRICE" on top, "OF HISTORY" below, same size.
- Keep the name on the tag, never on top of the coin, so it stays readable.
