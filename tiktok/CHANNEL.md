# Hidden Lines — channel rules

TikTok channel, English, aimed at US/UK viewers. Goal: Creator Rewards Program (videos over 1 minute).

- **Niche:** how everyday things work. One object per video, one hidden thing that makes it work.
- **Style, forever (from video 2):** our own **Night Paper Cutaway** (rules in `earbuds-anc/style/STYLE.md`, based on Lemo-Opuscar's Line-Art Cutaway): dark charcoal card, cream lines, objects built from stacked cut-card slices, paper motion (fold-ups, pull-tab slides), one orange accent for the path being explained. Video 1 used the original white Line-Art Cutaway.
- **Story:** every video opens and closes on a small cut-paper story (a person, a moment); the explanation is in the middle.
- **Format:** vertical 1080×1920, 24 fps, 60–90 s (must be over 1 minute to earn).
- **Language:** English narration, English-only subtitles.
- **TikTok safe zones:** keep text out of the bottom ~420 px (caption, username), the right ~150 px (buttons) and the top ~140 px.
- **Hook:** the puzzle in the first 2 seconds.
- **No brand names or logos** (generic objects only).
- **End card:** title block with "HIDDEN LINES" and the episode number.
- **Narrator:** calm, low American male (Kokoro `am_` voice), the same one every video.
